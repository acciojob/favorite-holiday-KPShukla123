Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh, known for its scenic mountains, cool climate, and adventure activities. It is a popular destination for tourists looking to relax and enjoy nature.

  1. Rohtang Pass – A breathtaking snow-covered mountain pass.
  2. Solang Valley – Famous for adventure sports like paragliding and skiing.
  3. Hadimba Temple – An ancient wooden temple surrounded by cedar forests.
  4. Old Manali – A peaceful place with cafes and culture.
  5. Beas River – Perfect for river rafting and scenic views.
  • Siddu – A traditional Himachali bread dish.
  • Thukpa – A hot Tibetan noodle soup.
  • Momos – Popular steamed dumplings.
  • Trout Fish – Fresh river fish delicacy.
  • Babru – A local snack similar to kachori.
